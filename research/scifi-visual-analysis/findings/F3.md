### [1] Matrix数字雨是模拟现实活动的动态视觉编码
- quote: "Digital rain, or Matrix code, is the visual effect of vertically falling green characters popularized by the 1999 movie The Matrix. The image is a way of representing the activity of the simulated reality environment of the Matrix on screen by kinetic typography."
- url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_rain
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-06-26
- confidence: high

- quote: "The code uses a custom typeface designed by Simon Whiteley, which includes mirror images of half-width kana characters and Western Latin letters and numerals. In a 2017 interview at CNET, he attributed the design to his wife, who comes from Japan."
- url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_rain
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-06-26
- confidence: high

### [3] 数字雨视觉风格借鉴早期绿色荧光显示器

### [4] Ghost in the Shell使用DGA技术实现赛博空间视觉化
- quote: "Ghost in the Shell used a novel process called 'digitally generated animation' (DGA), which is a combination of cel animation, computer graphics (CG), and audio that is entered as digital data. In 1995, DGA was thought to be the future of animation."

- published: 2026-07-19
