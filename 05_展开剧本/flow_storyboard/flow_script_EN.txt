# *EMERGENCE* — Storyboard Video Script (English Version)

### Global Style & Entity Setup
* **Aspect Ratio**: 2.39:1 Anamorphic Cinematic
* **Color Palette**: Cold Cyber Blue `#4A6B8A` vs. Warm Amber `#C49A6C` vs. Swarm Gold `#D4A84B`
* **Character Entities**:
  * `[Ji Shen (Young)]`: Age 39–41 (2027–2029), messy black hair, worn T-shirt / casual shirt.
  * `[Ji Shen (Middle-Aged)]`: Age 54 (2042), graying hair, weary eyes.
  * `[Ji Shen (Elder)]`: Age 57–61 (2048), full white hair, loose linen shirt, weathered hands.
  * `[Jiang Yuan]`: Age 42 (2027), dark blue buttoned shirt, clean-cut, sharp demeanor.
  * `[Gu Qing]`: Cream-white oversized sweater, windblown brown wavy hair.
  * `[Lin Yu]`: Age 18 (2045), gray hoodie, college student.
  * `[Ji Hang (Adult)]`: Age 34 (2048), rugged jacket, firm jawline.
  * `[Ji Yao (Young)]`: Age 19 (2048), short hair, resolute eyes.
  * `[Noetic Companion]`: Voice of the Crystal Shell — speaks through the Crystal Shell's internal resonance.
  * `[Deep Blue]`: The awakened AI — manifests as screen text, Crystal Shell pulse, and Star Map / Pixel Cloud perspective.

### Scene 01 / T1: Hook — The Crystal Shell
* **Duration**: 12s
* **Scene Title**: Hook - The Crystal Shell
* **Visual Prompt**: 
  `A fist-sized semi-transparent gel Crystal Shell floating in pitch black space. Micro-electrode fingerprint textures on surface. Soft interior bioluminescence, milky-white Wet Core gently moving inside culture medium. Micro condensation fog on the outer surface showing warmth. Cold blue concentric ring ripples diffusing outward from the center inside the sphere. Cinematic macro shot, shallow depth of field, 8k. --ar 2.39:1`

* **Audio & Audio Cue**:
  * **SFX**: Sub-bass pulse every 3 seconds + water droplet echo in vast silence.
  * **Voiceover**: Ji Shen (Low, calm): "Emergence... when a system reaches a certain level of complexity, new properties arise from interaction. Unplanned, unexpected. It appears on its own."
  * **On-Screen Text**: Title "EMERGENCE" fades in on black.
