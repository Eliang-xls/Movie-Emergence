# 《涌现》Google Flow Storyboard 视频脚本

### Global Visual Style & Prompt Preset（全局风格预设）
> **Visual Style**: Cinematic 2.39:1 anamorphic ratio, DCI-P3 color grading, photorealistic, cyber-oriental aesthetic, 4K film texture.
> **Color Palette**: Cold blue-grey `#4A6B8A` (cyber/protocol) vs. Warm amber `#C49A6C` (wetware/life) vs. Golden `#D4A84B` (swarm awakening).

### Scene 01 / T1：钩子 — 晶壳 (Hook - Bio-chip)
* **Duration**: 12s
* **Visual Prompt**: 
  `A fist-sized semi-transparent sphere gel bio-chip (wetware) floating in pitch black space. Micro-electrode fingerprints texture on surface. Soft interior bioluminescence, milky-white organ core gently moving inside translucent solution. Micro condensation fog on the surface showing warmth. Cold blue concentric ring ripples diffusing outward from the center inside the ball. Cinematic lighting, macro shot, shallow depth of field, 8k. --ar 2.39:1`
* **Camera & Lighting**: 
  * **运镜**: 纯黑屏开场 2 秒 → 镜头冷凝水汽微距近景 → 缓慢后拉（Slow Pull-back）揭示球体完整全貌。
  * **光影**: 纯黑背景，半透明球体内部淡蓝光纹与发光湿核。
* **Audio & Dialogue**:
  * **SFX**: 3秒/次的极低频脉动声（Sub-bass pulse）+ 水滴落入寂静的回声 + 古琴微弱泛音。
  * **Voiceover**: 季深（低沉平静）：“涌现……系统复杂到一定程度，新的性质就从交互中冒出来。没人设计，没人预期。……它自己出现。”
  * **Title**: 黑屏浮现白色字幕「涌现」。
