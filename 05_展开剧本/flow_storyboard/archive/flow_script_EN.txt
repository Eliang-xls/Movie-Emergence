  * `[Crystal_Shell]`: Fist-sized semi-transparent gel sphere (resembling an infant's fontanelle), micro-electrode fingerprint texture on outer surface, internal milky-white organic Wet Core floating in culture medium, subtle warm condensation fog on outer surface, mounted on a dark gray silicon base with blue LED pulsating rings.
